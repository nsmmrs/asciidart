[[art-6,Article 6]]
=== Article 6: Lawfulness of processing

[.paragraph]
[[art-6-1,Article 6(1)]]1. [[art-6-1-s1]]Processing shall be lawful only if and to the extent that at least one of the following applies:

[.point]
[[art-6-1-a,Article 6(1)(a)]](a) the data subject has given consent;

[.point]
[[art-6-1-f,Article 6(1)(f)]](f) processing is necessary for the purposes of the legitimate interests.

[.paragraph.resumed]
[[art-6-1-s2]]Point <<art-6-1-f,(f)>> of the first subparagraph shall not apply to processing carried out by public authorities.

[.paragraph]
[[art-6-2,Article 6(2)]]2. [[art-6-2-s1]]Member States may maintain more specific provisions.
